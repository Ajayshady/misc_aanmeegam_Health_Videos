##### Ingredients:
* Potato
* Cucumber
* Aloe vera gel
#### Preparation:
* Take a potato, cucumber, and some aloe vera gel.
* Grind all the ingredients together into a smooth paste.
* Extract the juice from the mixture.
* Pour the juice into an ice cube tray and freeze it.
####  How to Apply
##### Method 1 – Ice Cube Application
* Wrap an ice cube in a clean, soft cloth and gently apply it to your face. Avoid holding it in one place for too long.
##### Method 2 – Face Toner
* If you have a cold or do not want to use ice cubes, apply the freshly prepared juice directly to your face as a toner using a cotton pad.
##### Method 3 – Cloth Application
* Soak a clean, soft cloth in the freshly prepared juice and gently place it on your face for a few minutes.
